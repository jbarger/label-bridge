# Label Bridge privacy policy

*Last updated: November 25, 2026*

Label Bridge is made up of an Android app and a Windows program. Together they let
you print shipping labels from your phone on a label printer connected to your own PC.

## Your labels and files

- The PDFs you print are sent **directly from your phone to your own PC over your
  home Wi-Fi**. They are never sent to us or to any server we run. We have no servers.
- The Windows program saves the PDFs and preview images only on your PC, in
  `Documents\Label Bridge`. You can delete them at any time.
- The app stores your PC's local network address, the pairing code, and the
  printer's name on your phone so it can reconnect. These stay on the device.

## Advertising

The Android app shows a small banner ad provided by **Google AdMob**. To show ads,
the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- app interaction and diagnostic data (for example, ad impressions and taps,
  crash and performance data).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for
your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any
time with **Ad privacy choices** on the app's main screen. On any Android device you
can reset or delete your advertising ID in **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

## Children

Label Bridge is not directed at children under 13.
